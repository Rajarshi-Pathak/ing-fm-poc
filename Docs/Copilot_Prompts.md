### Impact of Legacy Coupon Roll-offs on Net Interest Expenses

As BASF's existing debt matures, these "legacy coupons" (the interest rates on the old debt) will roll off. In the current market environment, where interest rates are generally higher than they have been in recent years, refinancing this maturing debt will likely occur at significantly higher coupon rates.

This means that as BASF issues new bonds or secures new loans to replace the maturing debt, their average cost of funding will increase. Consequently, their net interest expenses will rise, directly impacting their profitability and cash flow. Proactive refinancing strategies, such as the proposed DCM transaction, are crucial to manage this transition efficiently, potentially locking in rates and optimizing the cost of debt before further market movements.
